### 3.4 **THE MECHANISM, AND IT IS SIMPLE**

`hdl/nec_test.sv:229` is the last line of the `CONF_STR` localparam:

```systemverilog
	"V,v",`BUILD_DATE
```

`CONF_STR` is the MiSTer OSD menu string, handed to `hps_io` as a parameter and
read out **byte by byte through a constant multiplexer**.  Two characters of an
~800-character constant change that mux's LUT mapping: **14,786 → 14,776 →

different design, and its **timing-driven** physical synthesis duplicates a
different number of registers on the binding arc — which on this tree is the
microcode ROM's own read loop, the most duplication-sensitive thing in it.

> **Nothing exotic, nothing per-opcode, no fitted table: one generated
> constant, one mux, one timing-driven optimiser.  The confusing behaviour was
> two simple systems interacting — a build stamp and a register duplicator —
> in a way nobody had looked at.**
